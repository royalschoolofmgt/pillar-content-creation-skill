## Key Takeaways for Content Strategy

1. **Content is the #1 gap** — All competitors have 50-{{C2_BLOG_POSTS}}x more blog content than {{BRAND_NAME}}. The {{BLOG_POSTS_COUNT}}-post blog is the biggest handicap to organic growth. A War-Time deployment of 100+ articles is needed.

2. **"Best of" and "Top 10" format dominates SERPs** — {{C3_NAME}}'s ~90 product guide articles are ranking for high-intent informational queries. This format should be the backbone of {{BRAND_NAME}}'s pillar content.

3. **{{C1_NAME}} proves small brands can rank with content** — Despite DA {{C1_DA}} (close to {{BRAND_NAME}}'s DA {{DOMAIN_AUTHORITY}}), {{C1_NAME}} ranks on page 1 with blog posts. Content investment pays off even at low DA.

4. **Own-brand e-liquids are a unique content angle** — No competitor has own-brand products at {{BRAND_NAME}}'s price point. This is an untapped content opportunity.

5. **Physical stores enable local SEO** — {{BLOG_POSTS_COUNT}} locations ({{HQ_NAME}} + {{STORE2_NAME}}) give {{BRAND_NAME}} a local SEO advantage that online-only competitors ({{C3_NAME}}) and single-store competitors ({{C1_NAME}}) cannot match.

6. **Competitor content weaknesses to exploit:**
   - {{C3_NAME}}'s guides aren't in their sitemap — opportunity to outrank them with properly indexed content
   - {{C2_NAME}}'s blog may have many outdated posts (low traffic despite {{C2_BLOG_POSTS}} posts) — fresh, current content wins
   - {{C1_NAME}}'s DA dropped significantly — opportunity to overtake with consistent content + link building
   - None of the competitors have strong video content strategy — YouTube opportunity

7. **Rewards/loyalty programs are table stakes** — All 3 competitors have them. {{BRAND_NAME}} should consider implementing one alongside the content strategy.
