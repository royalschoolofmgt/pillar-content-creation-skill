## Key Observations & Opportunities

### Strengths

1. **Exceptional review profile** — {{TP_RATING}}/5 on Trustpilot with nearly {{TP_COUNT}} reviews + {{GOOGLE_COUNT}} Google reviews + Lipscore 5.0/5. This is genuine, earned social proof that outperforms many competitors on trust.
2. **Aggressive pricing & own-brand strategy** — Own-brand e-liquids at £1, shots at £0.50, multibuys from £4/unit. Price leadership in a price-sensitive market.
3. **Excellent Core Web Vitals** — All green (LCP {{CWV_LCP}}, INP {{CWV_INP}}, CLS {{CWV_CLS}}). No technical speed barriers to ranking.
4. **Strong product catalog** — {{PRODUCTS_COUNT}} products across {{COLLECTIONS_COUNT}} collections. Comprehensive coverage of e-liquids, kits, pods, coils, accessories, and major brands.
5. **Physical presence** — Two retail locations ({{HQ_NAME}}, {{STORE2_NAME}}) with same-day pickup. Omnichannel capability that pure-play online competitors lack.
6. **Proactive regulatory content** — Vape tax transition communications show the brand is transparent and ahead of industry changes.
7. **Multiple trust signals** — Product authenticity page, TPD compliance page, age verification, BNPL, DPD shipping, live chat + WhatsApp support.

### Weaknesses

1. **Near-zero SEO content** — Only {{BLOG_POSTS_COUNT}} blog posts. No educational, buying guide, comparison, or informational content. This is the #1 opportunity gap.
2. **Very low domain authority (DA {{DOMAIN_AUTHORITY}})** — Only {{REF_DOMAINS}} referring domains. Competitors have DA 30-60+.
3. **Only {{RANKING_KEYWORDS}} ranking keywords** — Minimal organic search footprint. Virtually no non-branded organic traffic.
4. **Weak homepage SEO** — Generic meta title and description. No keyword targeting on the most important page.
5. **No About Us page** — Missing a fundamental trust/brand page. Contact page is the only brand identity page.
6. **Content published in wrong {{PLATFORM}} section** — Educational pages under /pages/ instead of /blogs/, losing blog archive and category SEO benefits.
7. **Google Business Profile incomplete** — No phone number, no website link on GBP. {{GOOGLE_RATING}}/5 rating (lower than Trustpilot {{TP_RATING}}) suggests some unmanaged negative reviews.
8. **No social media strategy** — Instagram handle references region (not brand-consistent). No TikTok, X, or LinkedIn.
9. **Missing schema markup** — No BreadcrumbList, WebSite, ItemList, or FAQ schema despite having content that could support these.
10. **Translation error on collection pages** — "Translation missing: en.products.product.regular_price" visible to users.

### SEO Content Opportunity

The gap between {{BRAND_NAME}}'s product quality/trust profile and its content/SEO footprint is massive. With {{PRODUCTS_COUNT}} products, {{TP_COUNT}} Trustpilot reviews, and near-perfect CWV scores, the site has strong commercial foundations but zero organic content strategy. This is the ideal scenario for pillar content architecture — the brand has credibility, products, and platform; it just needs content to capture search demand.
